About

I'm a Computer Science student at Handong Global University, working at the intersection of software engineering and human-computer interaction.

Most recently, I've been researching AI interaction as an undergraduate intern at the Ultimate Interface Lab, UNIST, advised by Prof. Seongkook Heo — looking at how people interact with AI agents, with a particular interest in accessibility.

Before that, my research centered on intelligent software engineering: LLM-based program repair, code graphs, and developer agents. I like taking a problem from its research question to a working system — designing the architecture, building the feedback loop, and learning from where it breaks.
