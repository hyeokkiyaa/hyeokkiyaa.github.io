About

I study how people understand, oversee, and collaborate with increasingly autonomous AI agents.

Currently, I’m an undergraduate research intern at the Ultimate Interface Lab, UNIST, advised by Prof. Seongkook Heo. My work sits at the intersection of human–computer interaction, AI agents, and software engineering.

My background is in automated software engineering, code analysis, and agentic systems. My current interests include the design and evaluation of interfaces that help people understand agent behavior and remain involved in decisions.

Research

CURRENT DIRECTION · HUMAN–AGENT INTERACTION

Interfaces for understanding AI agents

I study how people monitor progress, make sense of agent behavior, and stay in control while AI works in the background. My current work explores task-adaptive, glanceable representations that provide meaningful signals without demanding constant attention.

Agent legibilityHuman oversightAdaptive interfacesGlanceable progress

GenProgressBar

Exploring how an interface can adapt its progress representation to a task and an agent’s observable work, helping people understand what is happening at a glance.
